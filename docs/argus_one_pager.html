Argus is a privacy-first, sandbox-first, surveillance-first browser for cybersecurity professionals and power users. Real Chromium engine, tamper-evident audit trail, BYOK AI arbiter, and a future-proof plug-port for autonomous agents. Open-source under GPL v3.

Architecture

Three pillars, one workbench.

Each layer is independent. Disable any one of them and the rest still does its job.

S

Sandbox

argus_sandbox

Per-mode profile directories, scoped cookies and storage, and zero shared state between Normal, Privé, and Coffre. The cage is structural, not cosmetic.

V

Surveillance

argus_surveillance

HMAC-SHA256 chain over every browsing event. Tamper-evident JSONL log, replay-resistant timestamps, chain-verify CLI. You can audit yesterday.

A

Arbitrage

argus_arbiter

BYOK AI in the loop — Claude, GPT, Gemini, or local Ollama. Verdicts (allow / warn / block) and confidence are written to the audit chain alongside the request that triggered them.

Differentiators

Why Argus.

Four things you will not find together in any other browser today.

Cyber-forensic by default

Every browsing event lands in an HMAC-anchored chain you can verify offline. Most browsers log nothing. Forensic browsers cost five figures and lock you in.

AI-native, BYOK

The arbiter is not a SaaS — you provide the key, you own the prompt, your traffic never crosses our servers. Local Ollama is a first-class option.

Vault Mode for banking

Mode Coffre will (V3) enforce a domain whitelist, hardened ciphers, and a per-form keystroke fence. The plumbing is already in place; the policy ships next.

GPL v3 transparency

Source on GitHub, including the cryptography choices, threat model, and open gaps. No closed binaries shadowing security claims you cannot inspect.
